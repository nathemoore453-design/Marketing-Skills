# UGC-Style Ad Video — "Unsupervised" Pet-Nat (Inspire Moore Winery)

**Product:** Inspire Moore "Unsupervised" Sparkling Cayuga White (Pétillant Naturel / Pet-Nat), Finger Lakes, NY
**Link:** https://inspiremoorewinery.com/product/pet-nat/
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, ~16–30s, captions-on (TikTok / Reels / Shorts / paid social)

---

## 1. Product & Brand Snapshot (for context)

- **Style:** Pétillant Naturel ("Pet-Nat") made via the Ancestral Method — bottled mid-fermentation, unfiltered, unfined, spontaneously fermented, very low sulfites.
- **Grape:** Cayuga White, Finger Lakes, NY.
- **Tasting notes:** Primary — orange blossom, creamsicle, pineapple. Secondary — vanilla, nutmeg. Bubbly, textured, unique.
- **Social proof:** Won "Best Pétillant-Naturel" at the 2024 New York Wine Classic.
- **Brand voice cues from the label:** "Unsupervised," hand-lettered script logo, rainbow gradient artwork with a figure throwing its arms up (freedom/wild energy), neck text reads "How Wild Is Wild."
- **Vibe to hit:** playful, a little irreverent, "I can't believe this is wine" discovery energy — not stuffy wine-snob tone.

---

## 2. Creative Concept

**Hook:** "POV: your friend hands you a wine called 'Unsupervised'" — leans into curiosity about the name + the fact that it's fizzy, which is unexpected for a "natural wine."

**Arc:** Curiosity → Reveal (it's fizzy/Pet-Nat) → Sensory payoff (tasting notes) → Credibility (how it's made + award) → Origin (Finger Lakes/Cayuga White) → Reaction → CTA.

**Why it works for UGC:** Starts with a relatable "POV" hook (high completion rate), uses the product's actual quirky branding as the joke/hook instead of inventing one, and stacks a real award as a pattern-interrupt before the CTA.

---

## 3. Two Production Paths

### Path A — Real creator films it (recommended for authentic UGC)

Hand a creator (or yourself) this shot list + line reads. Film selfie-style on a phone, natural light, casual home/kitchen/porch setting. Each "scene" below = one take, ~2–4 seconds, jump-cut style editing (common UGC pacing).

### Path B — AI-generated b-roll / avatar (if no creator available)

Use the prompts in the table below with Veo 3 or Sora 2 (both support synced dialogue/audio) for talking-to-camera shots, or Kling/Runway for product b-roll only (no AI-generated faces saying brand claims — verify any AI avatar script against TTB/alcohol ad compliance before publishing).

## 5. Captions, Music & Editing Notes

- **Captions:** Bold sans-serif, white fill + dark stroke, centered top-third (matches the rendered teaser). Keep each card on screen 2–2.5s minimum for readability.
- **Music:** Pick a trending upbeat/playful track (TikTok Sounds or Reels Audio) — avoid anything moody/serious; the brand's "Unsupervised"/"How Wild Is Wild" energy wants something fun and a little chaotic.
- **Pacing:** Jump cuts every 2–3s. No single shot longer than ~4s.
- **Text-free product shots:** Per AI video best practices, don't ask AI models to render label text — use the real product (or the cropped product photo) for any close-ups where label text needs to be legible.
- **Compliance:** Any awards/claims ("Best Pet-Nat," tasting notes) should be verifiable on the product page/award listing before publishing as paid ad copy. Standard alcohol-ad disclaimers (drink responsibly, age-gate on landing page) apply per platform policy.
